About

I am a Doctoral Researcher in Data Science at the Web Science Lab, IIIT Bangalore, working at the intersection of intervention modeling, policy analytics, and sustainable development.

My research focuses on moving beyond descriptive dashboards towards prescriptive, stability-aware policy support systems that help policymakers translate data into actionable, context-sensitive interventions — grounded in the 'Logic of Being' framework and the Sustainable Intervention Score.

My PhD thesis, "Representation and Intervention Modeling for Sustainable Development Goals", was successfully defended in May 2026 under the supervision of Prof. Srinath Srinivasa.
