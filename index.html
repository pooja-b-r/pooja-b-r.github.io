About

I am a Doctoral Researcher in Data Science at the Web Science Lab, IIIT Bangalore, working at the intersection of intervention modeling, policy analytics, and sustainable development.

My research focuses on moving beyond descriptive dashboards towards prescriptive, stability-aware policy support systems that aid policymakers translate data into actionable, context-sensitive interventions.
